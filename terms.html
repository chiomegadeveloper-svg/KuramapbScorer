The service

Dinkykong Pickleball Scorer lets you show a pickleball scoreboard on a TV or tablet and control it from your phone. Using the scoreboard requires an active pass.

Passes and payment

  • Packages: PHP 50 for 24 hours, PHP 150 for 3 days, PHP 300 for 1 week, PHP 800 for 1 month.
  • Payment is made by InstaPay QR. After paying you submit the last 6 digits of the reference number. The owner checks each payment manually; approval takes 5 minutes to 2 hours.
  • Pass time starts when you first scan a scoreboard after approval and runs continuously until it ends.
  • Payments support the further development of the app.

One account, one phone

A pass belongs to the Google account that bought it and works on one phone. Passes cannot be shared, sold or moved to another account. If you change phones, contact support to reset the phone lock.

Refunds

If your payment cannot be verified it will be rejected and you can contact support. Approved passes are generally non-refundable; contact dinkykongsupport@gmail.com if something went wrong and we will review it.

Fair use

Do not misuse the service, submit false payment references, or try to bypass the pass system. We may suspend accounts that do.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. We work to keep it running but cannot guarantee it will always be available or error-free, including on every TV browser or network.
