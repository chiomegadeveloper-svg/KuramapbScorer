The service

Dinkykong Pickleball Scorer lets you show a pickleball scoreboard on a TV or tablet and control it from your phone. Using the scoreboard requires scoring time, which is unlocked by a donation to the DinkyKong support fund.

Support fund (donations)

  • Donation options: PHP 50 for 24 hours, PHP 150 for 3 days, PHP 300 for 1 week, PHP 800 for 1 month.
  • Donations are sent by InstaPay QR. After donating you submit the last 6 digits of the reference number. The developer confirms each donation manually; scoring time unlocks within 5 minutes to 2 hours.
  • Scoring time starts when you first scan a scoreboard after approval and runs continuously until it ends.
  • Donations go to the DinkyKong support fund, which pays the app’s bills for storage, domain and database, keeps it online and helps it grow. Each donation unlocks scoring time as a thank-you.

One account, one phone

Scoring time belongs to the Google account that donated and works on one phone. It cannot be shared, sold or moved to another account. If you change phones, contact support to reset the phone lock.

Refunds

If a donation cannot be verified it will be rejected and you can contact support. Confirmed donations are generally non-refundable; contact dinkykongsupport@gmail.com if something went wrong and we will review it.

Fair use

Do not misuse the service, submit false donation references, or try to bypass the scoring-time system. We may suspend accounts that do.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. We work to keep it running but cannot guarantee it will always be available or error-free, including on every TV browser or network.
